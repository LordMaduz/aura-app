Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

Aura ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information We Collect

Aura stores the following data locally on your device only using Apple's standard UserDefaults storage:

  • Your selected goals and age range (from onboarding)
  • Your name (optional, entered during onboarding)
  • Quote reading history and streak data
  • Saved favourite quotes
  • Notification preferences
  • App appearance preferences

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

In-App Purchases

Aura offers an optional premium subscription ("Aura Premium") processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment information. Subscription management is handled by Apple.

Notifications

Aura may request permission to send you local notifications (daily quote reminders). These are generated on-device and do not involve any external servers. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

Third-Party Services

Aura does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Children's Privacy

Aura does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL.
