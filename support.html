Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my premium subscription?

Open Aura → Profile (avatar icon) → Restore Purchases. Or open the paywall and tap "Restore Purchase".

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to iPhone Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Aura → Cancel Subscription.

Why isn't my streak counting?

A streak counts when you read at least one quote per day. Open the Ideas tab and scroll through at least one quote to register your daily read.

How do I change my notification time?

Profile → Notifications → tap the time on any notification card to adjust.
